1. Introduction

Welcome to Mewati Song Player ("we", "our", "us", "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

By using Mewati Song Player, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Contact: mewatisongplayer@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Identifier: When you first install the App, we generate a random anonymous device ID (UUID) and store it on your device. This is used to remember your likes, favorites, and preferences. It does NOT identify you personally.
  • Usage Analytics: We collect anonymous playback information such as which songs are played, play counts, and general listening activity. This helps us improve the App.
  • Device Information: Operating system version, device model, and app version — used for crash reporting and bug fixing.
  • IP Address and Approximate Location: When you connect to our servers, your IP address and approximate geographic location (country, region, city) are logged for analytics and abuse prevention.

2.2 Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Feedback and Suggestions: If you submit feedback, bug reports, or song suggestions, we store your message and optional contact information.
  • Voice Search Input: If you use voice search, your voice is processed locally on your device or via your device's built-in speech recognition. Voice recordings are NOT transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2.3 Information Collected for Singer Registration (KYC)

If you apply to register as a singer, we additionally collect:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number (10-digit)
  • Government-issued ID document photograph (for identity verification)
  • Live selfie photograph (for liveness verification)
  • Consent agreement metadata (timestamp, device information)

This information is stored securely and used solely for identity verification and fraud prevention.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving the App's functionality
  • Remembering your likes, favorites, and downloads
  • Processing singer registration applications
  • Sending responses to your feedback
  • Analyzing usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Complying with legal obligations

4. How We Store Your Information

  • Device ID and Preferences: Stored locally on your device using encrypted storage.
  • Likes, Favorites, and Analytics: Stored on our secure cloud infrastructure (Cloudflare D1 database).
  • Singer KYC Documents: Stored in encrypted Cloudflare R2 object storage with restricted access.
  • Crash Reports: Processed via Sentry, a third-party error monitoring service.

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services. Anonymous analytics data may be retained for up to 90 days. Singer KYC records are retained for legal compliance purposes.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party services (Cloudflare for hosting, Sentry for crash reporting) that help us operate the App.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government request.
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Mewati Song Player, our users, or the public.

You have the following rights:

  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information.

To exercise these rights, contact us at mewatisongplayer@gmail.com.
