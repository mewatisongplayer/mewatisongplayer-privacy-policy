1Introduction

Welcome to Mewati Song Player ("we", "our", "us", "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and delete your information when you use our Android application.

By using Mewati Song Player, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Contact: mewatisongplayer@gmail.com

2Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically collected information (all users)

  • Device Identifier: When you first open the App, we generate a random anonymous device ID (UUID) and store it on your device. It is sent to our servers to remember your likes and favorites and to count listening. It does NOT contain your name, phone number or any other personal detail.
  • Listening Activity: Which songs you play, how many seconds you listen, and the category of the song. We use this to show trending songs, to improve the App, and to calculate singers' earnings.
  • Approximate Location: When you connect to our servers, your IP address is seen by our hosting provider (Cloudflare). From it we record an approximate location (country, region, city and approximate coordinates). We do not access your phone's GPS and the App does not ask for location permission.
  • Device and Crash Information: Operating system version, device model, app version, and technical details of crashes or errors, used for bug fixing (see Section 10, Sentry).

2.2 Information you provide

  • Feedback and Suggestions: If you send feedback, we store your message, the category you pick, the song (if any) you refer to, and your device ID. Please do not include sensitive personal details in the message.
  • Custom Song Request ("अपना गाना भरवाएँ"): If you use this feature, we collect your name, mobile number, the singer you choose, the requested song length, and any shayari or lyrics you type. We store this on our servers and the App then opens WhatsApp on your phone with a prefilled message to the chosen singer. The message is sent only if you press send in WhatsApp, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to it. The singer will see your name, mobile number and request.
  • Voice Search: If you use voice search, the App uses the microphone and your phone's built-in Android speech recognition service. We do not record, receive or store your voice. Depending on your phone, the speech recognition provider (for example Google) may process audio under its own privacy policy.

2.3 Singer registration and singer account

If you register as a singer, we additionally collect and store:

  • Your name and 10-digit mobile number.
  • A 6-digit PIN that you choose. We store only a salted, hashed form of the PIN, never the PIN itself.
  • A record of your acceptance of the Singer Terms: the terms version and text, the date and time, your IP address, basic device information, and a verification hash. This is kept as legal proof of consent.
  • Songs you upload (audio files and cover images), their titles and categories, and your optional profile photo.
  • Login sessions (secure login tokens that expire after 30 days) and failed-login counters used to block guessing of PINs.
  • Listening and earnings statistics for your songs, which are linked to an anonymous singer ID.
Public display: For singers, your name, profile photo and approved songs are visible to all App users. Your name and mobile number are also shown in the Custom Song Request singer list so that listeners can contact you, as described in the Singer Terms.

"Remember PIN" on your phone: If you choose this option, your mobile number and PIN are saved only on your own phone, in Android's encrypted secure storage. They are not sent anywhere by this feature, and they are erased when you log out or delete your account.

6Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We share information only in these limited cases:

  • Service Providers: Cloudflare (hosting, database, file storage) and Sentry (crash reporting), which help us run the App.
  • Singers: If you send a Custom Song Request, the singer you choose receives your name, mobile number and request details.
  • All App users: A registered singer's name, photo, songs and (in the Custom Song Request list) mobile number are visible to every user of the App.
  • WhatsApp: Only when you choose to send a request through WhatsApp.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government request.
  • Safety: To protect the rights, property or safety of Mewati Song Player, our users, or the public.

8Your Rights, Choices and Account Deletion

You have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt-Out: You can stop using the App at any time and uninstall it.
🗑️ Open the account deletion page

🗑️ 8.1 Singers: delete your account inside the App

A singer can delete their account at any time: open Singer Profile, tap खाता हटाएं (Delete account), and confirm with your PIN. What happens:

  1. Right awayYou are logged out everywhere and your saved PIN is erased from your phone. Within a few minutes, you and your songs disappear from the App, public lists, rankings and the Custom Song Request list.
  2. 30-day grace periodFor 30 days we keep your data as it is. If you log in again with the same mobile number and PIN within these 30 days, the deletion is cancelled and your account and songs come back.
  3. After 30 days without login: permanent deletionThis cannot be undone.
    • Your PIN and login-attempt records are permanently deleted.
    • Your name is replaced by "Deleted singer", and your bio, profile photo and contact details are erased.
    • Your mobile number is anonymized, so the same number can be used to register again later.
    • Your profile photo and any pending upload files are deleted from our storage, and any song of yours still waiting for review is cancelled.
    • Your name and mobile number (as the singer) are removed from Custom Song Requests addressed to you. The details of the listener who sent a request are not changed by this; they are deleted on request (see Section 8.2).
What we keep after deletion: the record that you accepted the Singer Terms (terms version, date and time, IP address and verification hash), kept without your name or mobile number as legal proof; and earnings and listening statistics that carry only an anonymous singer ID and no personal details.
Your songs: they are removed from the App as described above, but their audio files are not deleted automatically from our storage. To have the files removed as well, email us at mewatisongplayer@gmail.com, as described in the Singer Terms.

8.2 Listeners and everyone else

Because the device ID is anonymous, we cannot look up your data by name. To ask for access or deletion of your data (including a Custom Song Request or feedback you sent), email us from the address you wish to use at mewatisongplayer@gmail.com, tell us what you want deleted, and include the mobile number you used (for custom song requests). We may ask for extra details to find your records. Note that deleting your device ID also removes your likes and favorites, since they are tied to it. We aim to reply within 30 days.

9Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS) for all data sent to our servers
  • Singer PINs stored only as salted hashes, with a temporary lock after repeated wrong attempts
  • Saved login details on your phone kept in Android's encrypted secure storage
  • Restricted access to our database, uploaded files and admin tools
  • Regular security updates to our infrastructure

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
