Delete Your Account & Data

This page explains how to delete your account and data from Mewati Song Player, an Android app by Mewati Song Player.

App: Mewati Song Player
Package: com.mewatisongplayer.app
Last Updated: 15 October 2026

1Singers: delete inside the App

If you registered as a singer, you can delete your account yourself, without contacting us:

  1. Open Singer ProfileOpen the Mewati Song Player app, go to Singer Profile and log in.
  2. Tap Delete accountTap खाता हटाएं (Delete account) and read the warning.
  3. Confirm with your PINEnter your 6-digit PIN to confirm.

What happens next

  • Immediately: you are logged out everywhere, and you and your songs disappear from the App, public lists, rankings and the Custom Song Request list.
  • 30-day grace period: for 30 days we keep your data as it is. If you log in again with the same mobile number and PIN within these 30 days, the deletion is cancelled and your account and songs come back.
  • After 30 days without login: your data is permanently deleted or anonymized, as listed in Section 4. This cannot be undone.
If you cannot log in (for example you forgot your PIN or no longer have the app), email us using the button in Section 3 and include the mobile number you registered with. We will verify and delete the account for you.

2Listeners: delete data linked to your phone

If you only listen to songs, the App has no login. Your likes, favorites and listening records are linked to a random anonymous device ID, so we cannot find them by name.

You can ask us to delete that data, and also a Custom Song Request or feedback you sent. Email us using the button in Section 3 and tell us what you want deleted. For a Custom Song Request, include the mobile number you used. We may ask for a few extra details to locate your records.

Uninstalling the App removes the device ID from your phone, but it does not delete the records already saved on our servers. Send us a request if you want those removed.

3Send a deletion request by email

Tap the button, or write to mewatisongplayer@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.
