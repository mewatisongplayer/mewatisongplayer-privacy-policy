Delete Your Account and Data

Mewati Song Player

Last Updated: 5 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and data from Mewati Song Player (Android app, package com.mewatisongplayer.app). See also our Privacy Policy.

1. Singers: delete your account inside the App

If you registered as a singer, you can delete your account yourself, instantly, without contacting us:

  1. Open the Mewati Song Player app and go to Singer Profile, then log in.
  2. Tap खाता हटाएं (Delete account).
  3. Read the warning and enter your 6-digit PIN to confirm.

This cannot be undone.

If you cannot log in (for example you forgot your PIN or no longer have the app), email us using the button in Section 3 from the request below, and include the mobile number you registered with. We will verify and delete the account for you.

2. Listeners: delete the data linked to your phone

If you only listen to songs, the App has no login. Your likes, favorites and listening records are linked to a random anonymous device ID, so we cannot find them by name. You can also ask us to delete a Custom Song Request or feedback you sent. Email us using the button in Section 3 and tell us what you want deleted. For a Custom Song Request, include the mobile number you used. We may ask for a few extra details to locate your records.

Uninstalling the App removes the device ID from your phone, but it does not delete the records already saved on our servers. Send us a request if you want those removed.

3. Send a deletion request by email

Email a deletion request

Or write to mewatisongplayer@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

4. What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Singer PIN, login sessions, login-attempt recordsPermanently deleted
Singer name, bio, contact detailsErased. Name is replaced with "Deleted singer"
Singer mobile numberAnonymized, so the same number can register again later
Singer profile photo and pending upload filesDeleted from our storage
Singer's songsRemoved from the App, public lists and rankings immediately. Their audio files are then deleted from our storage. Songs waiting for review are cancelled.
Your name and mobile number in Custom Song Requests addressed to a deleted singerRemoved
Listener device ID, likes and favoritesDeleted on request. Your likes and favorites are lost with it.
Custom Song Requests and feedback you sentDeleted on request
Record that a singer accepted the Singer Terms (terms version, date and time, IP address, verification hash)Kept, without name or mobile number, as legal proof of consent
Earnings and listening statisticsKept with only an anonymous ID and no personal details, for accounting
Live listening sessions and short-term countersDeleted automatically (about 90 days and about 1 day respectively)
